Every SaaS founder eventually faces the same tension: chase growth, or protect margin? The **Rule of 40** is the simplest lens investors use to judge whether you're getting that balance right. Add your annual revenue growth rate to your EBITDA margin - if the sum clears 40%, you're considered financially healthy. Fall short, and it's a flag worth explaining.

A 2025 BCG benchmarking study of 107 growth-stage B2B SaaS companies, run with Susquehanna Growth Equity and other funds, found that outperformance isn't random. The strongest predictor is the intersection of **gross revenue retention** (how much recurring revenue you keep) and **new-customer contract size**. Companies that retain customers well *and* sell efficiently - the "keepers" - post the best Rule of 40 scores. Those with the opposite profile, high-value deals paired with weak retention, struggle the most.

For founders, the takeaway isn't the score itself - it's the four levers behind it: manage retention deliberately, resist premature expansion, get real ROI from R&D, and scale sales only once it's efficient. Worth understanding well before an investor brings it up.

Full report: [Rule of 40: Lessons from the Top Performers in Software](https://www.bcg.com/publications/2025/rule-of-40-lessons-from-top-performers-software) (BCG, May 2025)
