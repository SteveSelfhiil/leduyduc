### Profile
My mission is to help software businesses in Southeast Asia grow sustainably through strategic investments, operational excellence, and innovation.

As Head of Business Development for Southeast Asia at Vela Software (a subsidiary of Constellation Software), I work with founders and leaders across the region to identify opportunities for growth and long-term partnership.

What makes Vela unique is our decentralized investment model — when we acquire a company, we empower it to operate autonomously, preserving its culture, leadership, and vision. Our role is to provide the capital, best practices, and global network that help these businesses thrive and scale — sustainably and on their own terms.

I'm passionate about collaborating with software industry innovators to ride the new wave of technology and business evolution — shaping resilient, customer-centric organizations that not only adapt but lead in a rapidly changing digital world.
