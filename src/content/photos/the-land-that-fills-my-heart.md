> My body was born in the sea, my heart felt in love with mountains, and my life is a journey back to happiness.
> — Le Duy Duc

![Vung Tau, the seaside town](/media/vung-tau-bai-truoc.jpg)

## Vung Tau

A young boy was born in the tranquil seaside town. Salty fresh air filled up his lung and bright sunlight darkened his skin. He grew up in peace and impeccable romance.

![Sai Gon](/media/sai-gon.jpg)

## Sai Gon

Food, motorbikes, traffic jam, business, study, network, energy, and whatever you can imagine and crave for could be found here. And the man has experienced more than half of his lifetime so far in this melting pot of exuberance.

![Ngoan Muc Pass, on the way to Da Lat](/media/ngoan-muc-pass-dalat.jpg)

## Da Lat

Love struck the second time and his heart is lost to now the mother of two angels. His wife adores the forest and so does he. Be it D'ran or Ngoan Muc Pass, he equally felt thrilled each time zigzagging their bending, stomach-churning roads.

![Ben Ninh Kieu](/media/ben-ninh-kieu.jpg)

> and happiness and beyond
> — is the next never-ending road and sea that he is travelling on…
